Prism is a local-first, repository-native workspace for organizing, understanding and presenting project knowledge.

## Product promise
Author a fact once. Connect it to its authoritative source. Render it in boards, tables, galleries, wikis, graphs and presentations without manual duplication.

## Initial customer
Prism's own development team (the owner and AI-assisted implementation). Emberfall remains the game-specific reference project.

## Success criteria
- Use Prism as the primary backlog and decision log.
- Trace a requirement to its implementation and verification.
- Produce a development review directly from current project records.
- Preserve user-owned files and work offline without a mandatory paid service.
